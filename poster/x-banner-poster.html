01

Introduction

Human affect recognition is a pivotal research area with direct impact on health & wellbeing, human–machine interaction and marketing. Among physiological signals, electroencephalogram (EEG) is extensively used to classify brain activity associated with emotions (Bastos-Filho et al., 2012).

Conventional pipelines rely on hand-crafted feature extraction and often neglect spatial representations of EEG topography. In this study we introduce a simple machine-learning model based on Spiking Neural Networks (SNN) with Hebbian-like learning that operates without manual feature extraction and preserves spike-timing information via Address Event Representation (Farabet et al., 2012).

Key contribution: SNN classifier discriminates Emotional Stress vs. Relaxation brain states at 83% average accuracy on unseen data — state-of-the-art without manual features.

03

Method  — Data, Encoding & Architecture

Dataset. DEAP (Koelstra et al., 2012) — 208 EEG samples balanced ( 104 Stressed + 104 Relaxed) selected via valence & arousal scores after music-video stimuli.

Pre-processing. 128 Hz downsample • EOG artefact removal • 4–45 Hz band-pass • pre-trial baseline removed → Data-averaging window → 252 × 32 × 208 tensor.

Encoding. Normalized samples encoded into spike trains using Address Event Representation (AER) (Farabet et al., 2012; Delbruck & Lichtsteiner, 2007).

Classifier. Single-layer SNN with 130 hidden neurons processing both inhibitory & excitatory spikes; Hebbian-like STDP learning; no manual feature extraction.

Results & Discussion

SNN classifier achieved 83% average accuracy on unseen data and ~80% cross-validation accuracy on training data. Compared to prior methods requiring extensive feature engineering, our model attains higher accuracy without manual feature extraction, confirming the merit of spike-based temporal coding.

Discussion — why it works
  • ●Spatial-temporal fidelity: AER preserves spike timing & channel topology vs. flattened feature vectors.
  • ●No feature tax: Hebbian-like learning discovers discriminative patterns end-to-end; ~11% above best feature-engineered baseline.
  • ●Balanced & generalizes: 83% unseen > 80% CV — no evidence of overfitting on 208 balanced trials.
05

Conclusion

This research delivers a state-of-the-art 83% accuracy for Stress vs. Relaxation classification without manual feature extraction — simplifying the pipeline and retaining spatial structure.

Future work: implement anti-STDP learning to reduce SNN training time and further optimize performance before web-platform deployment.
